01 — Overview

Celestial Summary

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by liquid water oceans — a rare feature in our Solar System.

Earth's protective magnetic field, generated by its rotating iron-nickel core, deflects harmful solar wind particles and cosmic radiation, creating conditions stable enough for life to flourish.

02 — Geology & Structure

Internal Composition

Earth's lithosphere is divided into roughly 15 major tectonic plates that constantly move, driven by convection currents in the mantle. This process builds mountains, triggers earthquakes, and fuels volcanoes.

The deepest point on Earth is the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench at 10,994 meters. The highest point is Mount Everest at 8,849 meters — a difference of nearly 20 kilometers.

Did you know?

"Earth is the densest planet in the Solar System. It's also the only planet not named after a mythological god or goddess — the name 'Earth' comes from Old English and Germanic words simply meaning 'ground'."

03 — Atmospheric Data

Planetary Envelope

Earth's atmosphere is uniquely suited for life: a precise mix of nitrogen and oxygen, with trace amounts of other gases, it regulates temperature, provides oxygen to breathe, and shields the surface from UV radiation.
