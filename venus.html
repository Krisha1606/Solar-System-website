01 — Overview

Celestial Summary

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and Earth's nearest planetary neighbor. It is strikingly similar to Earth in size and mass, but is an utterly alien and hostile world.

Venus rotates backwards compared to most planets, and its day is longer than its year. The sun rises in the west and sets in the east on Venus.

02 — Geology & Structure

Internal Composition

Beneath its dense cloud cover lies a young volcanic surface, covered with thousands of volcanoes, some potentially still active. The planet has vast highland regions and massive lava plains.

Unlike Earth, Venus lacks tectonic plates, meaning that heat escapes through periodic, catastrophic episodes of resurfacing that repave almost the entire planet in one massive volcanic event.

Did you know?

"Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System, with a surface temperature of around 465°C — hot enough to melt lead. This heat is trapped by its thick, runaway greenhouse atmosphere."

03 — Atmospheric Data

Planetary Envelope

Venus has the densest atmosphere of all the terrestrial planets, choked with 96% carbon dioxide, causing a runaway greenhouse effect. Surface pressure is 92 times that of Earth — equivalent to being 900 meters underwater.
